2. What is P(Surv) and Why is it on the Short List?

P(Surv) is your Survival Probability: the estimated mathematical likelihood that opponents will leave this player available until your upcoming snake pick.

  • Under 25% (High Snipe Risk): Almost certainly drafted before your next turn. If you want this player, you must draft them now.
  • 25% to 65% (Toss-up / In play): Active competition zone. Compare their positional cliff against alternatives.
  • Over 75% (Safe Sleeper): You have market leverage. You don't need to waste an early pick on them because consensus drafters will ignore them until later rounds.
⚡ Dynamic Cliff (VONA)
The drop in VORP from this player down to the next best available player at that position. A drop of +15 to +50 means passing on them costs you irreplaceable starting points.
📊 Adj VORP (Roster Utility)
VORP adjusted for your roster needs. Full 100% value for starting spots; automatically dampened by 15%–35% once starting spots at that position are filled to prevent wasteful bench stockpiling.
